## A. The Everyday Unfairness Fix

```
You [ordinary action] and [frustrating thing happens].

It can [emotional consequence]. Especially when you were [doing the right thing].

[Scale number] [people] [hit this] last year. Too many of them [were caught out by something they couldn't control].

[That's / It's] not fair. So [I'm / we're] changing it.

[What changes, with a specific limit or figure].

And if [edge case], [what happens now]. You'll just need [simple requirement].

Because when you're [doing the right thing], you don't deserve to be [ripped off / punished].

That's one less thing to [echo the emotional consequence from line 2].
```

## B. The Personal Story

```
My [relative] [name] had [specific object / place].

[One concrete scene from their life, with a place name].

Years later, [what the system did to them].

I [watched what it did to X], and I made a promise to [fight for something better].

That was [N] years ago. If anything, it's worse today.

I know so many of you have lived this.

[Shared experience 1]. [Shared experience 2]. [Shared experience 3].

I won't let that carry on.

So [what you're building / doing].

[One or two concrete details or numbers].

And I won't stop until it's fixed.
```

## C. The Round-up

```
On their own, some of the [fixes] I've [shipped] might not seem like much. But together they all add up.

Here's a reminder.

I'm [verb]ing [thing 1] - [short benefit].

I'm [verb]ing [thing 2].

I'm [verb]ing [thing 3].

And I'm [verb]ing [thing 4].

On their own, I know these don't [solve the big problem]. And there's a lot more I want to do.

But they do add up, and they matter to [people].

A lot done so far. But a lot to do. I'm getting on with it.
```

## D. The Milestone

```
[N] [days/months] ago I [started X] and promised to [do things differently].

I promised to [promise 1], to [promise 2], and to [promise 3].

There were plenty of people who told me this would be impossible. But [N] [months] in, [we're proving them wrong].

Already, [progress on the big thing, with numbers].

We've started [tackling the thing everyone else ducked].

And while we've had one eye on [big issues], we've kept the other on [everyday annoyances]. [List of 4–6 small fixes].

I'm not pretending the job's done. Not even close.

This is just the beginning. So stick with [me / us].
```

## E. Addressing Concerns Head-On

```
It just doesn't make sense to [status quo], when we could [alternative].

You'll have seen that [decision].

I know some of you will have concerns and questions, so I wanted to address them directly.

[Decision] will [benefit 1], [benefit 2] and [benefit 3 with a number].

But I want to reassure you that [I remain committed to the thing they're worried about].

The truth is, [hard reality].

That's why [I'm / we're] taking a common sense approach.

Because this is how we can:

[Aim 1].

[Aim 2].

And - crucially - [the thing people care about most].
```
